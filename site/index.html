Your computers,
in the room with you.

Command Center is a VR desktop for the Steam Frame. The Frame's own windows and the monitors of your other computers become panels you can put anywhere, and one mouse and keyboard drive all of them.

Install it with one command, on the Frame and on each computer you want to see

curl -fsSL https://framecc.nomadsgalaxy.com/install | sh

The installer works out which machine it's on, checks every download against the release's checksums, and walks you through pairing. Run it again to update or remove.

What it does

Here's what it does today. It's all Rust, on the Frame and on your computers.

Your monitors, where they really are

Align shows a few tags on each monitor and finds it with the headset's camera. Each panel lands on the real screen, usually within a few millimetres.

Every window is its own panel

The Frame runs its own Plasma session, and each window gets a panel you can carry, resize and curve. Drag a browser tab from one window into another, like on a normal desk.

One mouse, one keyboard

Point at a panel and you're driving that machine. Click a panel and your typing goes there. Drags carry across monitors of the same computer.

Workspaces that travel

Each room keeps its own layout. Away from home, load your home setup in front of you, and your real home layout stays untouched.

Paired, not SSH'd

A computer pairs with your Frame once, with a 6-digit key on its screen (or just look at it). After that it's TLS and a per-Frame login, and no SSH anywhere.

Close it and play

Closing the Desktop saves which apps were open and frees its memory for a game. Open it again and they come back.

Setup

It takes a few minutes per machine. The first build on the Frame is the slow part.

  1. On the Frame, open Konsole in desktop mode and run the install command. When it's done, open Desktop from the SteamVR launcher.
  2. On each computer, run the same command. Tick the monitors the Frame should show.
  3. Pair it. Run cc-share pair on the computer, then on the Frame open Workspace, Machines, Add machine, and type the key or look at the screen.
  4. Align it. Press Align in the Machines window and move your head a little. The panels snap onto your real monitors.

What you need

MachineRequirements
Steam FrameSteamOS with SteamVR. Command Center builds itself in a container there, so nothing gets installed into the system.
ComputersLinux with KDE Plasma 6 on Wayland and krdp, x86_64 or aarch64. macOS and Windows hosts are on the roadmap, once the Frame side is stable.
NetworkThe Frame and your computers on the same local network.
